The Aura
Protocol

Every rule, in the order you actually meet them — from the pre-market checks through the entry decision to what you draw on the chart. Built to be run the same way twice, until it is automatic.

What this page is not

  • It claims no edge. No hit rate, no win rate, no expectancy appears anywhere, because none has been established. The strategy in rules.md has never actually been backtested against its own quality rules — enforcing them takes the sample to n = 0.
  • No price data here is invented. Every candle is a real exported bar from Tradezella session 831607. Every gap is definitional arithmetic on those bars, reproducible by anyone holding the same export.
  • No range, SMT, bias or target is drawn on a real chart. Those detectors were found wrong on 15 Aug 2026 and are still under repair, so they appear here only as schematics of the rule — never as a claim about a specific day.
  • dOoMeR's hedges survive. Where he states a preference rather than a rule it renders as a soft branch and stays one. A decision tree wants to turn every hedge into a gate; this one is built to refuse.
01

Work a session

Nineteen steps, in the order the protocol runs them — set the chart up, frame the higher timeframe, draw down to the entry, decide, manage, review. At each step you are told what to draw and which tool to draw it with, and the chart beside it shows that step performed on a real session. Mark it on your own chart, tick it, move on.

02

The session, top to bottom

Eight phases, worked in order. A hard gate is a question whose wrong answer ends the trade — it is surfaced, never enforced: the page will let you tick past it and will keep showing you that you did. Ticks are yours alone and are kept in this browser.

03

The entry decision

The model is already an if-then machine — "a great way to eliminate your impulses… is to think like a machine." Answer each node and the path you took builds downward. The seven hard gates below are taken verbatim from the engine's own HARD_GATES list, in the order it evaluates them, so the tree and the code cannot drift apart.

This tree never ticks a rule for you. Answering a node highlights the rules that just came into scope in §01 — it does not check them. A box you did not consciously tick is not adherence, and manufacturing it would corrupt the only honest measurement here.

Fri 30 May: the execution, and the fourteen it did not take

Every blue box is an inverse FVG in premium that qualified on size. Only the solid amber one was traded. Seeing the rejected candidates beside the taken one is most of what makes the read reviewable.

Neither the stop nor the target is on this chart, and that is worth sitting with. The stop is 225.75 points away — wider than this entire day's range — and the target is roughly 615 points below the entry. Drawing either would flatten every box above into a hairline. One resolution cannot hold both a month-long range and a five-minute gap; that is the cascade problem, not a rendering bug, and it is the same fact the 27%-of-range flag is pointing at.

The gap's whole life, formation to entry. It forms at 20:30 the evening before and is retested at 08:05 the next morning. The traded zone is 21,341.75 – 21,343.25 — 1.5 points, six ticks. That is the width of the thing being retested, and on the day view above it is about two pixels tall. The precision this model asks for is not rhetorical.

What the record flags on its own face — reported, never resolved into a clean answer:

  • This is not the first retest. Price first returned to the inverted zone at 29 May 20:40, outside the 08:00–16:00 entry window, so the 08:05 touch taken here is a later one. does not say whether a re-tested iFVG is still valid, and the entry window is what made the earlier touch invisible.
  • The entry is before 09:30. dOoMeR prefers waiting for the open, particularly while consistency is still developing. is soft — but a strict 09:30 filter would delete this trade entirely.
  • The 15m confirmation diverges on CHFUSD alone, with no index leg agreeing, on the leg whose tick size is the least reliably measurable of the four. is itself flagged; this is the weakest link in the chain.
  • The stop is 27% of the range, over the declared 25% flag. 's answer is the same idea on a correlated leg with a tighter gap — not taken automatically.
  • No weekly-cycle SMT in the lookback, so this lacks 's canonical weekly-with-daily-inside structure. Daily→4H still satisfies it, but this is the weaker form. A measured zero, not a blind spot.
  • Neither range extreme was swept on every triad leg, and a materially different all-legs-swept candidate exists for each. prefers the all-legs-swept extreme; the engine reports both and does not re-anchor. The rulebook contradicts itself here.
Outcome · UNRESOLVED-AT-DATA-EDGE The trade ran 1.20R in favour and 0.41R against, and then the bars stopped — the replay is parked at 30 May 16:59 ET, confirmed at 1-minute resolution on all three futures legs. Neither the stop nor the target was reached. This is a measurement gap, not a breakeven and not a loss, and it must never enter a win rate.
