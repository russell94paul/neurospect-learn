## Declared thresholds — every one of these is a choice, not a fact

- **Noise floor:** 2 ticks of each instrument's own **measured** tick size, applied to BOTH sides of a divergence. Without it, zero-margin near-misses manufacture phantom SMT (measured 2026-08-13).
- **Forward window** (was the level taken?): 5m→8 bars, 15m→8 bars, 60m→8 bars, 240m→6 bars, D→5 bars
- **Cascade (R27):** framing `D` → confirm `240m` → session `15m` → entry `5m`
- **Range framing (R4/R7):** anchored on the driving SMT, searched from 10 framing bars before its pivot; SMT itself found over a 60-bar lookback
- **Entry window:** 08:00–16:00 ET — opens before 09:30 deliberately, so R31's pre-open branch stays reportable instead of being defined away
- **R34 oversize-stop flag:** risk > 25% of the range
- **R9 ambiguity tolerance:** 15% — inside this, the engine refuses to pick a range
- **CHFUSD admissibility (R17):** intraday only (1m–240m). **Refused at daily/weekly** — its bars bucket on a different exchange day, which is the exact defect R17 rejected DXY for.

**Hard gates** (a failure here is logged as a rejection naming the rule):
- `R3   swing must be SMT-qualified across the triad`
- `R18  SMT must nest across >=2 adjacent cycles (or R24 Sequential Skip)`
- `R6   the framed range must still be LIVE - a candle CLOSE beyond a boundary kills it`
- `R7   the driving SMT must still be current - a later same-cycle or opposing SMT retires it`
- `R30  a 5m iFVG must exist in the bias direction`
- `R35  the target must lie BEYOND entry in the bias direction`
- `R29  invalidation must be definable (a stop level must exist)`

**Soft / reported branches** (never gates):
- `R5/R32  entry in discount (long) / premium (short) — reported, not enforced`
- `R31     pre-09:30 ET entry — reported as a branch (soft rule)`
- `R9      ambiguous range — emitted as UNRESOLVED, never silently picked`
